enat team website — updated local prototype

FILES
- index.html: full studio website preview (entry point).
- studio-preview.html: full studio preview with the glowing halo, glossy NOT NEED / 没必要 feature card, and locked sections.
- not-need.html: dedicated film detail page, synopsis, and three locked behind-the-scenes cards (projects, behind, cast).
- assets/: supplied logo and background image assets.

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Keep the HTML files and assets folder together.
2. Open index.html or studio-preview.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Scroll to the films section and click the glossy NOT NEED / 没必要 card.
4. On the film page, the first section is the synopsis; the second is 幕后花絮 with locked projects, behind, and 演员表 cards.
5. Locked items open the Coming Soon dialog. Press Escape or click outside to close it.
